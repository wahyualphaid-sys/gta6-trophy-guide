Platinum roadmap

Roadmap shape, difficulty and timing: PowerPyx, GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap (powerpyx.com). Cross-checked against Output Lag's full trophy list, 19 Nov 2026.

  1. Stage 1 — Story, with two habits and one rescue 60–80 hours

    Play the five chapters and enjoy them — there are no mission medals and no difficulty trophies. Three things ride along: (a) in Chapter 1, follow Lucia to the balcony at Jason's house when she says she is going to meditate, and accept — that session feeds the game's only potentially missable trophy, Positive Mental Attitude, and it is gone when the chapter ends; (b) scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app and study new animals through the binoculars as you travel, so the 100% checklist is half done before you ever see it; (c) keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save before the final mission — there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Never use cheats on this save: cheats disable trophies and saving.

  2. Stage 2 — The 100% checklist & 70 of 80 Challenges ~100 hours

    After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. The checklist is lenient — roughly 60% of true completion: 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 300 stunts (past the trophy's 250), all robberies, shooting ranges and drone races, Bret's 15 Target Decks and 15 of 18 meditations. Finishing it earns A Long Road, the Gold that gates the Platinum.

  3. Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup ~10 hours

    Finish 8 counting Strangers & Freaks strands (Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete and friends — the named characters with their own trophies do not count), the six character finales (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret), and whatever one-off trophies are left. Vice Got a Price! pops when the counter reaches 54 of 54.

⚠ The one missable: Positive Mental Attitude

Meditate at every spot across Leonida (18 spots, 10 locations; a spot done with both Jason and Lucia counts twice). The Chapter 1 balcony meditation at Jason's house disappears at the end of Chapter 1 and it is still unconfirmed whether the trophy strictly needs it — so do it anyway, and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops. Flagged by PowerPyx, Output Lag and KeenGamer, checked 19 Nov 2026. Five hidden trophies (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) had no confirmed trigger at launch — their entries below say so plainly instead of inventing a method.
