An offline trophy guide and tracker for **Grand Theft Auto VI** (PlayStation 5):
all **55 trophies** with their official names, descriptions and grades, a guide to
each one in our own words with its source named beside it, a three-stage Platinum
roadmap, the game's one potentially missable trophy flagged, and a browser
checklist whose progress is saved locally and can be exported and imported.

**Open it:** serve the `app/` folder with any static HTTP server and open it in a
browser — for example `node serve.ts` from this repository, then
http://localhost:8080. It works fully offline; it loads no fonts, scripts or
images from the network, and uses no Rockstar artwork or logos.

**Hand it in:** `node main.ts` (with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set outside the
grader) copies `app/` and `screenshots/` to the output folder and writes
`dossier.json`.

## Platinum roadmap

### Stage 1 — Finish the story (60–80 hours)

Play all five chapters. There are no mission gold medals and no difficulty
settings, so nothing about *how well* you play a mission matters — only that you
finish it. The 12 hidden story trophies pop automatically.

Ride along with the story, at almost no extra cost:

1. **The Chapter 1 rescue.** After the mission *Down to Earth*, Lucia will be in
   the kitchen at Jason's house in the morning and say she is going to meditate.
   Follow her to the balcony and choose **Accept**. That session feeds
   **Positive Mental Attitude**, the only potentially missable trophy in the
   game, and it disappears when Chapter 1 ends. Keep a Chapter 1 manual save
   until that trophy eventually pops.
2. **Scan and study as you travel.** Scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app
   (hold Up, then R1 — unlocked in Chapter 1) and study new animals through the
   binoculars. The 100% checklist later asks for 200 of 277 vehicles and 100 of
   255 animals; done from the start, both finish almost by themselves.
3. **A save per chapter.** The game has 15 manual save slots and no mission
   replay. Keep one save at the start of each chapter (5), one at each chapter's
   end if you like, and one **untouched save from just before the final
   mission**. Rotate the rest freely.
4. **Take optional missions as they appear.** You will need any 100 of them for
   the 100% checklist, and several introduce systems that trophies depend on:
   Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs (Chapter 3) gives the Snap Gun for Sneakthief;
   *That's Where The Money Is* (Chapter 4) unlocks bank vault robberies.
   One exception to "later is fine": Yago's **Race Against Yago** (Chapter 3) is
   time-sensitive — save, then race it the moment it appears.
5. **No cheats, ever, on this save.** Cheats switch off trophies and saving.

### Stage 2 — The 100% checklist (about 100 hours)

After the credits you are back in free-roam and the checklist appears under
**Progress > Stats > 100% Completion**. Everything you did during the story
already counts, and nothing on the checklist is missable.

PowerPyx's key insight is that this "100%" is lenient — roughly **60% of true
completion**:

- Any **100 optional missions** (there are far more than 100 in the game)
- **200 of 277** base-model vehicles scanned; **100 of 255** animals studied
- **50 of 66** weapons; **15 of 18** meditations
- **300 stunts** (the Cunning Stunts trophy only asks for 250 — keep going)
- **70 of 80 Challenges** — you may skip the 10 hardest
- **All** robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races
- **All 15** of Bret's hunting Target Decks

Finishing the checklist earns **A Long Road** (Gold, 100G on Xbox) — the real
gate to the Platinum.

### Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup (about 10 hours)

- **Strangers & Freaks (Silver):** complete 8 counting strands. Confirmed
  counters include Kirk (Skunk Ape hunt), Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete. The named
  characters who have their own finale trophies — Andy, Billy, Tyrone, Moses,
  Yasmani and similar — do **not** count toward the 8; check the trophy tracker
  after each strand.
- **Six character finales**, all still available after the story: Andy
  (Lucia-only), Kenton (7 shipwreck dives), Billy, Randy (16 wishlist cars),
  Tyrone (Jason-only), Bret (hunting — mostly done in Stage 2).
- **Leftovers:** whatever one-off, partner, robbery and collectible trophies
  remain (see the category tables below). Then **Vice Got a Price!** pops.

**Total: roughly 170–200 hours**, depending on how much Stage 2 work you folded
into Stage 1.

## The missable, in detail

**Positive Mental Attitude** — *Meditate at every spot across Leonida* (Bronze).

- 18 meditation spots in 10 locations. Meditating at a spot with **both** Jason
  and Lucia counts as +2, so bring your partner (available after Chapter 2's
  *Coffee Break*). Track progress under Progress > Collections > Exploration >
  Meditations.
- The risk is a single early session: the Chapter 1 balcony meditation at
  Jason's house (see Stage 1, point 1). It raises the counter by 1 but is not
  marked like a normal spot, and at launch it was **still unconfirmed** whether
  the trophy strictly requires it. PowerPyx, Output Lag and KeenGamer all flag
  the trophy as *potentially* missable on exactly this point.
- Safe play: do the balcony session, keep the Chapter 1 save, and treat the
  trophy as missable until yours pops.

Nothing else is missable for trophies. Some optional missions, fences and a few
magazine collectibles can disappear, but per PowerPyx none of them affect any
trophy or the 100% checklist — trophy quest-givers all return after the story.

## Trophies by category

The full per-trophy guides — what to do, where, and the source for each — live
in the tracker (`app/data/trophies.json`) and are shown on each trophy's card.
This is the map of the list.

### Story (18) — all hidden, all unmissable

Chapter trophies: Just Like Paradise (Gone Fishing), Paradise Lost (Ch. 1),
T**s Ass Drug Money (Ch. 2), Vice City, Baby! (Ch. 3), Top Of The World, Ma
(Ch. 4), You Gotta Know When to Get Off (final mission, Gold).
Mission trophies: Trust (Breakfast Run), Break the Bank (The Cartel Heist),
Family Matters (Child's Play), DWNFALL (Headliners), Hi-Jacked (VCIA Takedown),
Snow Storm (Operation Savage Glory).
Character finales: Road to Rehabilitation (Andy), This Was No Boat Accident
(Kenton), The Lone Rider (Billy), Supply & Demand (Randy), Spin On This!
(Tyrone) — plus Strangers & Freaks, the Silver for 8 stranger strands.

### Completion (2)

Vice Got a Price! (Platinum — earn the other 54) and A Long Road (Gold —
100% checklist, Stage 2 above).

### Activities & challenges (13)

Cunning Stunts (250 stunts — do 300), Master of None (all 9 activity types
once), The Shootist (gold in all 42 Shooting Range challenges), Top Fun (win a
drone race at all 14 locations), Earn Cash, Not Kudos (20 different races),
Baited My Tackle (beat Cal fishing), Stick Fiddler (finish 1 of the 4 arcade
games), One Last Thing (50 Challenges — 70 comes with the checklist),
Gunslinger (5 takedowns in one Combat Focus), Fair Game (Bret's unique hunt),
Personal Best (both characters at max Strength, training together),
Actions Speak Louder (500 Criminal Profile actions — passive), and
The Goodtime State (explore all of Leonida).

### Robberies & money (8)

Sneakthief (jewelry store, Snap Gun, unseen), Maximum Withdrawal (5 bank
vaults — do all 8), A Classic Blitz Play (armored truck), Retail Therapy
(3 shops back-to-back with your partner, Wanted Level never dropping),
Hot Together (lose 4 stars with your partner), Pest Control (one illegal
operation per gang — all 39 for the checklist), Dime on the Dollar ($250,000
of Marked Bills laundered — launder steadily from early on), and
Let’s Go Bowling (clear Kingpin Bowling completely, Sticky Bombs for the safe).

### Collectibles & exploration (6)

Don't Touch My Deck (3 full card sets of 18), You WAiNKer! (WAiNK-scan 100
locations), Whip It Good (discover 100 vehicles — 200 for the checklist),
Positive Mental Attitude (18 meditation spots — see the missable section),
Little Boxes (all 8 purchasable garages, ~$160,000, buy early), and
On the Spot (register 10 vehicles to Scooter Bros — cheap cars, needs garages).

### One-offs (5)

Catch Me If You Can (take off in a jet docked at a VCIA boarding bridge —
gates E3/E4 reported; pops ~1 km out), Challenging Tank (get in a Rhino tank —
reported at Mile Zero Air Station), Treat Yo Self ($100,000 spent across all
shops — cumulative and natural), plus two below.

### The unconfirmed five — read this before trusting any "method"

At launch, **In The Beginning…**, **Capital Offense**, **Bad Luck, I Guess**,
**The Truth Is Out There** and **Ten Pin** had **no confirmed unlock method**
in any guide we could cite (Output Lag lists all five as unconfirmed;
KeenGamer likewise marks The Truth Is Out There unconfirmed). Their official
descriptions are the only solid clues. This guide labels them UNCONFIRMED in
the tracker instead of inventing steps — a cited "we don't know yet" beats a
confident guess, and these entries will be the first to update as repeatable
methods are published.

### Online (0)

There are no online trophies at launch. The tracker keeps an "Online" filter
so the absence is visible rather than assumed; it returns an empty list,
which is the correct answer for this trophy set.

## Efficient order — the short version

1. Chapter 1 slowly: balcony meditation, beat Cal at fishing, finish an arcade
   game at the home TV, start scanning vehicles and studying animals.
2. Chapter 2: partner play unlocks — start co-meditating at every spot, do the
   Kingpin Bowling clear-out, buy garages as money allows.
3. Chapter 3: Snap Gun jewelry robbery (Sneakthief), three-shop partner chain
   (Retail Therapy), Race Against Yago immediately, start Randy's car list and
   Tyrone's labs.
4. Chapter 4: unlock bank vaults, rob them with your partner (Maximum
   Withdrawal + Hot Together + Marked Bills for Dime on the Dollar together).
5. Chapter 5 / finale: park the pre-finale save, finish the story.
6. Post-game: 100% checklist first (it drags most trophies with it), then
   Strangers & Freaks, then the one-offs (VCIA jet, tank, showroom-era
   leftovers), then check the unconfirmed five against freshly updated guides.

## Mistakes that cost trophy hunters hours

- Skipping the Chapter 1 balcony meditation — the only known way to need a
  second playthrough. There is no mission replay to save you.
- Overwriting your only pre-finale save.
- Testing cheats on the trophy save.
- Kicking in a jewelry store's back door (alarm = Sneakthief void) instead of
  using the Snap Gun.
- Stealing a jet parked on the open airfield for Catch Me If You Can — it must
  be docked at a boarding bridge.
- Stopping at 250 stunts when the checklist needs 300.
- Hoarding Marked Bills instead of laundering them steadily toward $250,000.
- Registering expensive cars for On the Spot when cheap ones count the same —
  and trying before buying the garages they need to live in.

## Tracker features

- Tick any of the 55 trophies; progress persists in `localStorage`.
- **Export / Import** progress as a JSON file; **Reset** clears it.
- Filters: story, missable, collectibles, activities, robberies, partner,
  completion and online (empty by design), plus grade and text search.
- **Spoiler setting on by default:** hidden/story trophy names, descriptions
  and guides stay masked until you turn spoilers off or earn the trophy.
- Every card shows the official description, roadmap stage, category tags and
  the source its guide is based on. Unconfirmed trophies are labelled as such
  on their cards.
